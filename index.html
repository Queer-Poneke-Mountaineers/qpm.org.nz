About Us

Welcome to Queer Pōneke Mountaineers, a tramping club in Wellington, New Zealand, dedicated to queer people and their friends and allies. We're a new club focused on outdoor adventures, hiking, and building community in the beautiful landscapes around Te Whanganui-a-Tara.

Whether you're an experienced tramper or just starting out, join us for safe, inclusive outings where everyone can feel welcome and celebrated.
